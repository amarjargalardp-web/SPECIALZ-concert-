SPECIALZ stage

Keyboard-driven backdrop visuals for the projector, with a live feed from a phone camera.

ComputerOpen on the laptop that drives the projector. Shows the visuals and the phone's code and QR code.Open the stage program → PhoneOpen on the phone that films the band. Sends its camera live to the stage program.Open the phone camera →
  1. On the computer, open the stage program, press F for fullscreen. The panel shows a 4-letter code and a QR code.
  2. On the phone, scan the QR code (or open the phone page and type the code), then tap Start camera and connect.
  3. Press C on the computer to put the phone on screen, V to change its look, X to mix it over the visuals.
